Any song

A file on your computer or a YouTube link. The voice is pulled out of the mix and becomes your reference.

Exact feedback

Where you went flat, where you rushed, and by how much: in cents and milliseconds.

Stays on your machine

Everything runs on your own computer. Your recordings never leave it.

How it works

One song, five steps, from first listen to finished mix.

  1. 01

    Reference

    Listen to the isolated vocal, the instrumental, or both with the voice turned down. Trim to the part you are working on and transpose it into your key. Lyrics are found for you and light up word by word as the song plays.

  2. 02

    Practice

    Exercises drawn from the song itself, its range, its long held notes and its leaps, played on a grand piano and scored as you sing them.

  3. 03

    Takes

    Record with the lyrics following along and a live pitch line tracing your voice against the original. Or bring a take recorded elsewhere, even a full mix with instruments.

  4. 04

    Compare

    Your pitch over the original, in cents, with note names alongside. Held notes are measured on their centre, so a scoop into a note is not mistaken for singing it out of tune.

  5. 05

    Export

    Your take placed on the original timeline and mixed with the instrumental at the level you choose, as MP3, WAV or FLAC.

Install

Download, open, and let it set itself up once.

macOS YOUR SYSTEM

APPLE SILICON (M1 OR LATER) · 16 GB MEMORY RECOMMENDED

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Vocalika into Applications.
  2. Open Vocalika. macOS says it cannot verify the developer: click Done.
  3. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  4. Allow the microphone when asked.
DOWNLOAD FOR MACOS →

Windows YOUR SYSTEM

WINDOWS 10 OR 11, 64-BIT · 16 GB MEMORY RECOMMENDED

  1. Run the downloaded setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer, then open Vocalika from the Start menu.
DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS →

The first start downloads the audio and machine-learning components, about 1.8 GB, and takes a few minutes; after that Vocalika starts in seconds and works offline, apart from fetching songs from YouTube. It needs about 3.5 GB of disk. The app is not yet signed by Apple or Microsoft, which is why the steps above are needed. On Linux, or to hack on it, run it from source.
